Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How is Advibe different from a cold email spam tool?

Advibe is an AI Relationship OS. It discovers warm paths first, validates investor mandates in real time, and keeps outreach strictly drafted until you review and approve it.

Where does your investor data come from?

We cross-reference 30+ verified institutional sources, SEC filings, portfolio announcements, and direct partner verification with real-time email validation.

Is my pitch deck kept confidential?

Yes. All company data is isolated with Postgres Row Level Security (RLS) and is never used to train public foundation models.
